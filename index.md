# ZVoice Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 5, 2026

ZVoice is a personal project operated by Zara A ("I", "me"). This policy explains how I handle information when you receive or send text messages through ZVoice.

## Information I collect
- Your mobile phone number
- The content of messages you send to or receive from ZVoice
- Your opt-in consent and opt-out requests

## How I use it
I use this information only to send you the messages you signed up for sign up verifications, respond to your replies, and keep a record of your consent.

## Sharing
**No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Information is shared only with my messaging provider, Twilio, to deliver messages, and where required by law. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

## Message frequency and rates
Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

## Opting out and help
Reply **STOP** at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply **HELP** for help, or contact me at wonderz@gmail.com.

## Retention and security
I keep message content for 30 days, after which it is deleted. Records of your opt-in consent and opt-out requests are kept for as long as needed to show compliance with messaging regulations. I use reasonable measures to protect this information.

## Changes
I may update this policy and will post the new date above.
